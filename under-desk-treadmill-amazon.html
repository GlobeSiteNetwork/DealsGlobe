❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are under-desk treadmills worth it?
Yes — studies show walking at 1-2mph while working increases calorie burn by 100-200 per hour with no measurable drop in cognitive performance. Most users report feeling more energised and focused.
What speed should I walk at while working?
1-2mph is the sweet spot for most desk tasks. At this speed you can type, read and take calls comfortably. Save 3-4mph for when you're on a call or doing simple tasks.
Do under-desk treadmills work with standing desks?
Yes — they're designed specifically for use with sit-stand desks. Most models are slim enough to slide under a desk at standing height. Check the height clearance spec before buying.
