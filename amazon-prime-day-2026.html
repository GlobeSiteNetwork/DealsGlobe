How to Prepare for Prime Day — 7 Steps

  1. Get Prime — You need Prime to access Prime Day deals. Start your free 30-day trial now at amazon.com/prime.
  2. Build your Wish List — Add everything you're considering to your Amazon Wish List weeks before Prime Day. You'll see price drops instantly.
  3. Track prices now — Use CamelCamelCamel to see if a "Prime Day price" is actually a good deal vs the regular price.
  4. Set deal alerts — In the Amazon app, set alerts on specific products. You'll be notified the moment the price drops.
  5. Check early deals — Amazon releases "early Prime Day deals" 1-2 weeks before the main event. Often just as good.
  6. Act fast on Lightning Deals — Lightning Deals sell out in minutes. Prime members get 30-minute early access. Have your payment info saved.
  7. Compare with other retailers — Walmart, Target and Best Buy run competing sales during Prime Day. Sometimes they beat Amazon's prices.

💡 The #1 Prime Day Mistake

Most people buy things on Prime Day just because they're on sale — not because they actually need them. Make your Wish List before Prime Day and only buy things already on it. You'll save money and avoid buyer's remorse.

What NOT to Buy on Prime Day

  • Groceries — Prime Day food deals are weak. Walmart beats Amazon on groceries year-round.
  • Clothing — Discounts exist but selection is limited. Better deals at brand sales.
  • Anything you're buying just because it's discounted — the biggest Prime Day trap.
  • Last year's tech at "big discounts" — often just clearance, not real Prime Day deals.

❓ Prime Day FAQ

When is Amazon Prime Day 2026?
Amazon Prime Day 2026 is expected in July 2026, typically running for 48 hours. Amazon has not yet confirmed the exact dates. Based on past years, the second week of July is most likely. Sign up for alerts above to be notified the moment dates are confirmed.
Do you need Amazon Prime for Prime Day?
Yes — Prime Day deals are exclusive to Amazon Prime members. You can sign up for a free 30-day trial to access all Prime Day deals without paying, then cancel after if you wish.
What are the best things to buy on Prime Day?
The best Prime Day deals are always on Amazon's own devices (Echo, Kindle, Fire TV) with discounts of 40-60%. Electronics, kitchen appliances, home goods and beauty products also see major price drops.
How much can you save on Prime Day?
Prime Day typically offers 20-70% off across thousands of products. Amazon's own devices regularly hit 50% off. The average shopper saves $100-$200 by planning purchases around Prime Day.
Is Prime Day better than Black Friday?
For electronics and Amazon devices, Prime Day often has better deals than Black Friday. Black Friday typically wins on clothing, toys and home goods from third-party retailers. Smart shoppers use both events.
