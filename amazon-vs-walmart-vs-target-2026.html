Why Amazon Wins for Most Shoppers

For the average shopper who isn't primarily buying groceries, Amazon wins on almost every metric that matters:

  • Price: Amazon's dynamic pricing means deals appear constantly. Lightning Deals can cut prices 40-70% for a few hours.
  • Selection: 300M+ products vs Walmart's ~75M and Target's ~30M. Whatever you're looking for, Amazon has it.
  • Shipping: Prime's 1-2 day free delivery is unmatched. Walmart and Target require a $35 minimum for free shipping.
  • Subscribe & Save: Amazon's subscription discount (up to 15%) on repeat purchases beats both competitors for consumables.
  • Reviews: Amazon's review system is the most trusted and comprehensive of any retailer.

💡 The Smart Move

Use Amazon for electronics, gadgets, books, toys, kitchen appliances and anything you'd normally research online. Use Walmart for groceries and household basics you buy in bulk. Use Target when you want stylish home decor or clothing — especially with a RedCard for the 5% discount.

When Walmart Actually Beats Amazon

  • Groceries: Walmart's grocery prices are consistently 10-20% lower than Amazon Fresh.
  • Same-day pickup: If you need something today and there's a Walmart nearby, in-store pickup beats any shipping.
  • Return window: Walmart's 90-day return window beats Amazon's standard 30 days.
  • Name-brand basics: Tide, Bounty, Charmin — Walmart often prices these 5-15% lower than Amazon.

❓ FAQ

Is Amazon cheaper than Walmart?
It depends on the category. Amazon wins on electronics, books, toys and niche products. Walmart wins on groceries and household basics. For most non-grocery purchases, Amazon's combination of price and Prime shipping makes it the better overall value.
Does Amazon price match Walmart or Target?
Amazon does not have a formal price match policy. However, Amazon's prices update dynamically and frequently. Use CamelCamelCamel to track Amazon price history and get alerted when prices hit your target.
Which store has the best deals on electronics?
Amazon consistently offers the best prices on electronics, especially during Lightning Deals, Prime Day and Black Friday. Amazon also stocks a much wider range of brands than Walmart or Target.
