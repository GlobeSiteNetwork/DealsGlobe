What to Consider Before Applying for a Loan

Borrowing can help cover an immediate expense, but a loan also creates a financial obligation. Review the terms carefully and decide whether the payments fit your budget.

1. Check the annual percentage rate

The annual percentage rate (APR) helps express the annualized cost of borrowing, including certain fees. Compare APRs and total repayment amounts rather than looking only at the amount you could receive.

2. Calculate the total repayment

Add up the scheduled payments and any applicable charges to understand what the loan will cost overall. Confirm when payments are due and what happens if a payment is late.

3. Review your monthly budget

Account for housing, utilities, food, transportation, existing debts, and savings. Do not assume future income or approval that has not been confirmed.

4. Check the lender's requirements

Eligibility rules, state availability, loan amounts, and terms depend on the lender and your circumstances. Verify current details directly with the provider before applying.

Consider the alternatives first. Ask your service provider about a payment extension, explore community assistance if eligible, or compare other credit products. The least expensive suitable option may not be a loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

General information to help you research borrowing options. Confirm product-specific details with the provider.

What is an installment loan?

An installment loan is typically repaid through a series of scheduled payments. The amount, frequency, duration, interest, and fees depend on the agreement.

How much does a loan cost?

Costs depend on the amount borrowed, APR, fees, and repayment period. Read the loan agreement and disclosures to determine the total amount you would repay.

Can I qualify for a loan?

Approval and eligibility depend on the provider's current requirements and your circumstances. This website cannot determine whether you will qualify or guarantee approval.

Availability and applicable terms may vary by state. Check MaxLend's official website and current disclosures for the rules that apply to your location.

Will visiting this website approve my application?

No. This website provides general information only. Visiting an external site or clicking an affiliate link does not guarantee eligibility, approval, or funding.

Are there alternatives to a short-term loan?

Depending on your needs, you may consider negotiating a bill payment plan, asking about an employer advance, contacting a credit union, or exploring local assistance. Compare costs and eligibility before choosing.
