The Break-Even Point

📊 When Does Prime Pay for Itself?

Standard US shipping without Prime costs $5.99 per order on orders under $25, or $0 on orders over $25. The average Prime shipping saving per order is approximately $6-8. At $14.99/month, you need roughly 2 orders per month to break even on shipping alone — before counting any streaming or deal benefits.

Honest Pros and Cons

✅ Pros

  • Free 1-2 day shipping pays for itself fast
  • Prime Video rivals Netflix for originals
  • Prime Day deals are genuinely excellent
  • Amazon Photos unlimited storage is a hidden gem
  • 30-day free trial with no commitment
  • Student version is half price
  • Annual plan saves 20%+ vs monthly

❌ Cons

  • Price increased significantly since 2022
  • Prime Video library smaller than Netflix/Disney+
  • Prime Music has less content than Spotify
  • Not worth it if you order less than once a month
  • Some "Prime eligible" items still have slow delivery

Who Should NOT Get Prime

  • You order from Amazon less than once a month — the shipping savings won't cover the cost
  • You already pay for Netflix + Spotify — Prime Video and Music become redundant
  • You only buy groceries — Prime Fresh has an additional fee in most areas

Who Should Definitely Get Prime

  • Anyone who orders online regularly — 2+ orders/month and it pays for itself
  • Students — Prime Student is half price with a 6-month free trial
  • Families — share with up to 2 adults and 4 teens under Amazon Household
  • Cord cutters — Prime Video replaces cable for many households
  • Deal hunters — Prime Day and Lightning Deal access alone can save hundreds per year
