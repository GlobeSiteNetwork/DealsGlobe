How Much You Save on Heating Bills

A heated blanket uses roughly 60-100W of electricity. At UK rates that's about 2p per hour. Running your central heating for the same warmth costs 10-15x more. UK households using a heated throw instead of the boiler for evening TV are saving £200-£400 per winter.

Safety — What to Check

  • Overheat protection — essential, listed in the product specs

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are heated blankets safe to sleep with?
Modern heated blankets with auto shut-off are safe for adults. Most switch off automatically after 1-3 hours. It's not recommended to sleep with one on all night — use it to warm the bed, then switch off.
How much does it cost to run a heated blanket in the UK?
At current UK electricity rates, a heated blanket costs approximately 2-4p per hour to run. That's around £1 for a full month of nightly use — far cheaper than running central heating.
