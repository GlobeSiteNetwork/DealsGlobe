❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most effective sleep aid on Amazon?
White noise machines are consistently rated the most effective non-supplement sleep aid. They mask disruptive sounds that cause micro-awakenings. The LectroFan is the highest-rated on Amazon with over 45,000 units sold monthly.
How much melatonin should I take for sleep?
Most sleep researchers recommend starting with 0.5-1mg, not the 5-10mg doses common in supplements. Lower doses are often more effective and avoid the grogginess the next morning.
Do weighted blankets actually work for sleep?
Yes — multiple studies show weighted blankets (typically 10-15% of your body weight) reduce anxiety and improve sleep quality, particularly for people with anxiety, ADHD or sensory processing differences.
